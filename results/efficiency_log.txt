Note the size of that range. How much a layout is worth depends strongly on
how fast you already type: at a slow sequencing rate the fingers always have
time to arrive and the layout barely matters, while at speed the same-finger
collisions become the binding constraint. Anyone quoting a single percentage
for a layout's benefit is hiding this.
