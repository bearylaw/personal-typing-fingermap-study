On QWERTZ, German text: capitals are 5.2% of letters, and using the
wrong Shift costs 79% more per capital. Over a whole document
that is 0.113 effort units per letter typed -- roughly
4.2% of total typing effort, for free.

==============================================================================
2. BACKSPACE
==============================================================================

Backspace is 57 mm from the right pinky's home key -- the longest reach
on the board. Modelled cost 6.30 effort units.
A thumb key (e.g. right Alt remapped) costs about 0.35.
Saving per press: 5.95 units.

  backspace as % of keystrokes   effort saved
---------------------------------------------
                          2.0%           4.4%
                          4.0%           8.8%
                          5.9%          13.0%
                          6.5%          14.3%
                          8.0%          17.6%

(Denominator is ~2.7 effort units per character of text, the QWERTZ average,
so these are savings relative to the cost of typing the text itself.)

Dhakal et al., 'Observations on Typing from 136 Million Keystrokes' (CHI 2018)
measured error-correction rates of 5.9% for trained and 6.5% for untrained
typists, KSPC 1.173, and found Backspace among the three most-pressed keys
alongside Space and 'e'. At those rates, moving Backspace to a thumb is worth
more than the entire letter-layout difference between ZEHN and AdNW -- and it
requires learning exactly one key.
